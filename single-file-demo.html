Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Give a range or a starting figure. A page that hides the price loses the visitors who were ready to buy.

How long does it take?

State the typical timeline and what would make it longer, so nobody is surprised later.

What do you need from me?

List it plainly. Most delays come from the client not knowing what to send.

What if it is not right?

Say what happens next — revisions, refunds, or how disagreements get settled.
